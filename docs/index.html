Engineering Excellence

Built for Speed. Zero Web Bloat.

Medha was built from the ground up in native Swift and SQLite to respect your hardware, battery, and cognitive bandwidth.

⚡

Native Swift 5.9 & SQLite WAL Architecture

No Electron. No Chromium background daemons. Medha starts up cold in under 120 milliseconds, uses less than 50MB of RAM at rest, and manages relational data through GRDB.swift SQLite 3.45 with high-concurrency WAL mode.

🛡️

100% Offline & Private

Zero cloud accounts. Zero tracking telemetry. Your notes and flashcards belong exclusively on your hard drive.

🕸️

GPU Knowledge Graph

Renders 1,000+ vertices at 60–120 FPS on Metal GPU canvas. Simulation cools to rest with 0.0% CPU drain when idle.

🤖

Private Local AI Synthesis

Synthesize note outlines and flashcards offline using compact local models (Qwen 2.5 / DeepSeek-R1) in <2GB RAM.

📦

Native Anki .apkg Importer

Seamlessly import legacy .anki2 and modern zstd-compressed .anki21 collections with built-in unzstd binaries.
